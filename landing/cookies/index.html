Cookie Policy

The short version

  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics) are only set if you accept them in the banner. We set no advertising cookies.
  • A small cookie remembers your choice for a year, and you can change it any time with “Cookie settings”.
  • The dashboard stores a sign-in session in your browser so you stay logged in.

What cookies and similar storage are

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Similar technologies, such as session storage, work the same way. We use them only where they are needed.

The website (funchole.dev)

  • No advertising cookies.
  • Session storage. The page remembers the GitHub star count for your visit, so it doesn’t ask GitHub again on every page. It is deleted when you close the tab.
  • Your choice. fh_consent stores whether you accepted or declined analytics, for one year. It is shared between funchole.dev and app.funchole.dev so we only ask once, and it is strictly necessary for the banner to work.
  • Analytics, only if you accept. We use Google Analytics. It sets cookies (such as _ga and _ga_*) to tell visits apart and measure how the site is used. By default they last up to two years.
  • Cloudflare. Our CDN may set cookies or similar data that are strictly necessary for security and performance, such as detecting bots and protecting against attacks.

The dashboard (app.funchole.dev)

When you sign in, the dashboard stores a session token in your browser so you stay signed in. It is necessary for the Service to work, so it doesn’t need your consent. Google sign-in may also set its own cookies on Google’s domains; see Google’s policies.

Third-party requests

The website loads Google Analytics from Google Tag Manager (only after you accept), loads fonts from Google Fonts and calls the public GitHub API. Those requests send your IP address and browser details to those services, and they may set their own cookies. See their privacy policies for details.

Changing your choice

Use “Cookie settings” in the footer of this site, or in the dashboard account menu, to accept or decline analytics again. Declining removes the Google Analytics cookies already set. The dashboard follows the same choice.

Managing cookies

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the dashboard’s session storage will stop you from staying signed in.
